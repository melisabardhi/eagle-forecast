Nested-EAGLE (ECMWF Anemoi Global and Limited-area Ensemble) is an AI-based numerical weather prediction model developed by the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA). It produces **10-day (240-hour) weather forecasts** for the contiguous United States (CONUS) at high resolution by nesting a fine-scale regional grid (HRRR, 6 km) inside a global grid (GFS, 0.25°). The model is designed for near-real-time operational forecasting, running every 6 hours aligned with standard GFS/HRRR initialization cycles (00Z, 06Z, 12Z, 18Z), and outputs forecasts made publicly available through the Microsoft Planetary Computer.

Nested-EAGLE is built on the **ECMWF Anemoi framework** (PyTorch), leveraging Flash Attention and requiring an Ampere-class GPU or newer (H100 confirmed). The model ingests 14 atmospheric variables across 12 pressure levels plus surface fields, producing a full atmospheric state forecast at each 6-hour lead time step. The checkpoint size is >1 GB. The model pipeline consists of three stages: (1) CPU-based preprocessing to download and regrid GFS and HRRR initial conditions, (2) GPU-based inference running 40 autoregressive steps to generate a 240-hour forecast, and (3) upload of NetCDF output to the Planetary Computer blob storage. Inference completes in approximately 10 minutes on a Standard_NC40ads_H100_v5 (H100 80GB) instance.
